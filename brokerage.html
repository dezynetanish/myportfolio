About EPF (Employee Provident Fund)

Key Features:

  • Mandatory: For organizations with 20+ employees
  • Employee Share: 12% of Basic + DA goes entirely to EPF account
  • Employer Share: 12% total - 3.67% to EPF, 8.33% to EPS (Pension Scheme)
  • Interest: Calculated monthly, credited annually, compounded yearly
  • Tax Benefits: EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) status - contributions, interest, and withdrawals are tax-free
  • VPF (Voluntary PF): Additional voluntary contribution beyond 12%, gets same interest rate
  • Withdrawal: Can withdraw after 2 months of unemployment or at retirement (age 58)
  • Partial Withdrawal: Allowed for specific purposes like medical emergency, house purchase, education
  • UAN: Universal Account Number stays with you across jobs

Note: This calculator provides estimates. Actual EPF balance may vary based on salary changes, withdrawals, and interest rate revisions.
